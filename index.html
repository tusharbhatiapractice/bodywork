What is bodywork?

Skilled, hands-on care for the body.

Bodywork uses touch, pressure, assisted stretching and slow, breath-led pacing to ease muscular tension, improve how freely you move, and settle an overworked nervous system. It is a complementary practice, not a medical treatment, and it works well alongside your doctor's or physiotherapist's care. Many of my clients are women, and the work is designed around their comfort, privacy and control.

Body

Releases muscular tightness and may ease stiffness, aches and restricted movement. Many people with long-standing neck, shoulder and back discomfort find real relief.

Nervous system

Slow, safe touch and steady breathing help the body move out of stress mode into rest. People often notice calmer breathing, less tension and deeper sleep.

Emotional release

When the body finally feels safe enough to relax, held stress and emotion often begin to move. Some people feel tearful, some feel light, some feel deeply calm. All of it is welcome, and nothing is required of you.

Awareness

Over a few sessions you start to notice where you hold tension and how you move, which helps the changes last.

If you live with long-standing pain or stiffness, bodywork can be a supportive part of your care. I work within your comfort, and I will always say when something needs a doctor's input first. Results vary from person to person, and it often takes more than one session to notice lasting change.

The Practitioner Philosophy

Listening to what your body holds.

I don't view myself as a guru or a healer coming in to "fix" you. Healing is simply what your body naturally does when it feels safe, supported, and unburdened.

When you sit on my mat, I don't follow a rigid sequence. I pay close attention to your posture, your breathing quality, where your muscles brace, and how your nervous system responds to touch. My daily life is rooted in breathwork, movement, martial arts, and inner practice, allowing me to show up present, sensitive, and grounded in every session.

I work intuitively, listening with my hands to what your body is holding and responding to it moment by moment. Many clients describe the sessions as deeply calming, and some experience them as spiritual or energetic. I leave that to your own experience, and no belief is needed.

Three Session Styles

Choose the experience that fits your needs.

Every session is individualised and adapted in real time based on your comfort.

Fully Clothed · No Oil

Flowing Energy Release

A dynamic, movement-led session combining passive stretching, gentle body-rocking, and targeted Marma point pressure. It feels like receiving the opening benefits of a deep yoga session without having to do any work yourself. Marma points are specific pressure points from the Indian Ayurvedic tradition, used here for gentle, targeted pressure.

Best for: Stiff joints, desk fatigue, or if you prefer to stay fully clothed.

Warm Oil · Fully Draped

Grounding Earth Bodywork

Slow, deliberate hands-on work using warm oil to soothe an overstimulated mind and soften hard, tired muscles. Strict, respectful draping ensures you remain completely covered and comfortable at all times.

Best for: Mental exhaustion, burnout, or deep rest.

Custom Blend

Harmony Flow

A tailored session bringing together the active movement of assisted stretching with focused oil-based soft-tissue work on priority areas like your neck, shoulders, upper back, or feet.

Best for: Addressing stiffness and nervous system calm together.

The Session Journey

What happens inside a session.

Sessions move smoothly between stillness and movement, shaped entirely by what your body needs on the day.

About the Practitioner

Tushar Bhatia

I'm Tushar. I started as a strength coach in a gym. Over the years I taught martial arts, ran group sessions, and trained corporate teams, events and tactical units. Movement was my work and my identity, and I saw the body as something to train and push.

Then a bike accident broke my leg and stopped everything. Lying in bed with nowhere to go, I started reading and listening: Sadhguru, Osho, Om Swami. They spoke about the body as something intelligent, not a machine. I began learning yoga and Ayurveda, and practising mantra, chanting and stillness, and I applied all of it to my own body. My life started to turn around.

What changed most was what I let go of. I stopped believing that more effort always means more results. I started resting, breathing, stretching and moving without a plan. Today, when so many of us are trying to do too much, bodywork is how I help people come back to themselves. Everything I've learned since 2012 is in how I work with you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Before you book.

Is this a regular spa massage?

No. While it uses hands-on touch, bodywork is more adaptive and observational. It looks at your entire movement pattern, posture, and nervous system state rather than running through a fixed routine.

Do I need to undress?

Not unless you choose an oil-based session. Flowing Energy Release is done fully clothed in loose athletic or yoga wear. For oil sessions, professional draping keeps you fully covered except for the area being worked on.

What should I wear?

For fully clothed sessions, comfortable stretchy clothing. For oil sessions, you are fully draped and only the area being worked on is uncovered at any time.

Will it be painful?

No. While deep tissue or Marma work can feel firm, it should never feel sharp or unbearable. We work strictly within your comfort boundary.

Is it safe if I have a medical condition or I am pregnant?

Often yes, with changes. Please tell me before booking. For pregnancy, recent surgery or a recent injury, I may ask for your doctor's guidance first, and I will say plainly if something is outside my work.

Can I book during my period, or after pregnancy?

During your period, yes. Tell me beforehand and I'll adapt the pressure and areas to what feels comfortable. For pregnancy or the months after a birth, please check with your doctor first and tell me when you book. I'll only go ahead with their go-ahead.

What do I need to provide for a home visit?

I bring a clean, padded floor futon, clean linens, and everything required. All you need is a clean, quiet room with open floor space and loose, comfortable clothing.

Is it normal to feel emotional during a session?

Yes, it's common. When muscles and breathing soften, feelings that have been held can surface. You can pause or stop at any time. Bodywork supports release and relaxation, but it is not a substitute for mental health care. If you're going through something heavy, I'd encourage you to also work with a counsellor or doctor.

What if I don't feel anything special?

That is fine too. Many people simply relax, loosen up and sleep better. There is no right way to feel, and every body responds differently.

How often should I get a bodywork session?

Some people come once to understand where their body is holding tension. Others book every 2 to 4 weeks to maintain ease in movement and manage ongoing stress. We discuss what makes sense for you after your first session.

Message me on WhatsApp and we confirm the time, place and session style. You can pay by UPI, bank transfer, or card, arranged on WhatsApp. Please give 24 hours' notice to reschedule.
